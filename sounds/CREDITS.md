# Sound credits

Every recorded sound in this folder is released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution isn't required, but here it is.
Each file was downloaded from the author's official page, then converted to 22.05 kHz mono WAV.

| Files | Pack | Author | Official page |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kenney_*` (footsteps, doors, coins, cloth, creaks, books) | RPG Audio | Kenney (kenney.nl) | https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio |
| `kenney_impact*`, `kenney_footstep_*` | Impact Sounds | Kenney (kenney.nl) | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds |
| `artisticdude_*` | RPG Sound Pack | artisticdude | https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack |
| `rubberduck_*` | 80 CC0 creature SFX | rubberduck | https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx |
| `yd_dungeon002` (music) | Dungeon Ambience | yd | https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-ambience |

Everything not listed in `sounds.txt` is synthesised by the game itself (src/audio_sfx.cpp,
src/audio_music.cpp).
